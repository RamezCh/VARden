# Code of conduct

VARden is a small project, and everyone who takes part in it (issues, pull requests, discussions) is expected to be kind and constructive.

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people. No insults, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- **Assume good intent,** and ask before assuming someone meant harm.
- **Help newcomers.** Many people find VARden because environment variables confused them. There are no stupid questions here.
- **Keep it on topic** and keep other people's private information private.

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, report it privately through **Security > Report a vulnerability** on the repository (it reaches only the maintainer), or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, or block people who don't follow this code.
